# Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure Template

This is a template for your privacy policy and cookie disclosure. Customize this according to your specific needs and consult with a legal professional.

## Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Idmize ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you visit our website.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### Information You Provide:
- Name and contact information
- Email address
- Message/inquiry content
- Any other information you voluntarily provide

#### Information Collected Automatically:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited
- Time and date of visits
- Referring website
- Device type and operating system

### 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:
- Remember your preferences
- Understand how you use our site
- Track ad performance and retargeting
- Personalize content

#### Types of Cookies We Use:

**Essential Cookies (Required)**
- Session management
- Security
- User preferences
- Form submission data
- Duration: Session

**Analytics Cookies (Optional)**
- Google Analytics
- User behavior tracking
- Performance metrics
- Duration: Up to 2 years

**Marketing Cookies (Optional)**
- Facebook Pixel
- Google Ads
- LinkedIn Insight Tag
- Remarketing/Retargeting
- Duration: Up to 1 year

### 4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and improve our services
- Respond to inquiries
- Send promotional materials (with consent)
- Analyze website usage
- Improve user experience
- Comply with legal obligations
- Track marketing campaign effectiveness
- Create targeted advertising audiences

### 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

**Analytics:**
- Google Analytics: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Analyzes website traffic and user behavior

**Marketing & Advertising:**
- Facebook Pixel: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy
- Google Ads: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Enable targeted advertising and remarketing

**Infrastructure:**
- Vercel/Next.js
- Database hosting services
- Email services

### 6. Cookie Consent

You have control over cookies:
- Essential cookies are required for site functionality
- You can opt-out of analytics and marketing cookies
- You can change preferences anytime
- Cookie preferences are saved for 1 year
- Withdrawing consent prevents future data collection

### 7. Data Retention

- Personal information: Until you request deletion
- Analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics)
- Marketing cookies: 365 days
- Website logs: 30 days

### 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location:

**GDPR (EU/EEA Users):**
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

**CCPA (California Users):**
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of data sales
- Right to non-discrimination

**To exercise rights:** Contact us at [your-email@domain.com]

### 9. Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:
- HTTPS encryption

- Limited access to personal information
- Regular security reviews
